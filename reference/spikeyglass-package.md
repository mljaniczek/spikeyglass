# spikeyglass: Bayesian Joint Spike-and-slab Graphical Lasso

Package implementing the method Bayesian joint spike-and-slab graphical
lasso to infer multiple Gaussian graphical models in R. Bayesian
treatments of group graphical lasso and fused graphical lasso.
